# Strawboat

![Strawboat](assets/strawboat-cover-v1.png)

A cozy game about catching arrows with a straw boat.

[Play in your browser](https://eric-schecter.github.io/straw-boat/)

## How to Play

- Hold the left half of the screen to turn left.
- Hold the right half of the screen to turn right.
- Release to stop.
- Catch enough arrows before the volley limit.
- Deflected arrows do not count. Protect your hull!
- **A/D or arrow keys:** Turn left or right.
- **P or Esc:** Pause or resume.
- **R:** Restart during play.
- **BGM ON/OFF:** Toggle background music.
